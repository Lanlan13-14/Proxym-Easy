# Domain sources

`all.txt` is regenerated by `scripts/gen-domains.sh` from the union of:

1. `../StreamConfig.yaml`, sourced from `lthero-big/Smartdns_sniproxy_installer`.
2. `1stream.txt`, a normalized FQDN-only snapshot derived from
   `1-stream/1stream-public-utils/stream.smartdns.list` at SHA-256:
   `b9b31491c0ab99aaa88ddcef7038e296f4275401dd838aaf07c005d4ff2d2ca1`.

Normalization removes comments, SmartDNS syntax, duplicates, IPv4 literals, and
non-FQDN entries such as the bare token `bbc`. The two sources are merged rather
than replacing one another because each contains valid domains missing from the
other.

Current counts:

- StreamConfig source: 522 domains
- normalized 1-stream source: 585 domains
- merged canonical set: 600 unique valid FQDNs
